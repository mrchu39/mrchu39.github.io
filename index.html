Hello. I'm Matthew.

I am a Physics Ph.D. student at the University of California, San Diego, specializing in Observational Cosmology. My objective is to apply my astrophysics and data analysis knowledge to deepen our understanding of the universe.

Observational Cosmology & Instrumentation

My current research with the Keating/Arnold Group at UC San Diego centers on the Simons Observatory. My primary work involves instrumentation for the SAT-I telescope, as well as replicating the analysis pipeline of the Simons Array.

Transient Facilities & Supernovae

Previously, I served as a Postbac Researcher for the Zwicky Transient Facility at UC Berkeley, automating transient classifications and analyzing galaxy concentrations for LIGO. Before that, I extracted spectropolarimetric data from Type Ia supernovae with the Prof. Alexei Filippenko Group and Lawrence Berkeley National Lab to better understand dark energy and galactic dust mechanisms.

Instruction & Space Engineering

Beyond active research, I have a passion for teaching, having served as a Graduate and Undergraduate Student Instructor for physics and cosmology courses. I also bring hands-on engineering experience from an electro-optical internship at the Space Dynamics Laboratory, where I calibrated equipment for the Roman space telescope.
